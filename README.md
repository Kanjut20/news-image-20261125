## The six stories (all events dated Wed 25 Nov 2026)

1. Pacific — Australia & Solomon Islands sign security treaty in Canberra;
   Australia becomes primary security partner, no foreign military bases.
   Sources: pm.gov.au statement; Reuters Sydney Nov 25.
2. Space — Shenzhou-24 launches Wed 25 Nov 08:18 Beijing from Jiuquan;
   crew Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi, and Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad
   Zeeshan Ali — first Pakistani in orbit, first foreign astronaut on
   Tiangong. Sources: Xinhua Nov 25; Reuters Nov 25.
3. Colombia — Special forces storm an Éxito supermarket in Cartagena at
   ~01:00 local Wed 25 Nov, freeing all remaining hostages after a siege
   that began Tue 24 Nov; 1 gunman killed, 4 detained. Framed as the
   25 Nov rescue, not the 24 Nov seizure. Sources: Reuters/AFP Nov 25;
   Colombia Reports; The Insight International.
4. War — Ukrainian drones strike Rosneft refineries in Russia's Samara
   region overnight into Wed 25 Nov; fires at Syzran & Kuibyshev, 1 killed,
   power cut. Sources: Reuters Nov 25 02:12 UTC; Kyiv Independent.
5. China — Shanghai party leadership pledges loyalty at a Wed 25 Nov
   meeting (Jiefang Daily front page), after the Chen Jining probe was
   announced Tue 24 Nov. Framed as the 25 Nov pledge only, not the probe.
   Sources: Reuters Beijing Nov 25.
6. USA — US Army to detonate ~5 tons (68 drums) of degraded lead azide at
   the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant, Middletown, set for Wed 25 Nov ~09:00
   CST; residents told to stay indoors. Scheduled 25 Nov event, same
   pattern as the 24 Nov image's scheduled Shenzhou panel. Sources: US
   Army announcement (EIN Presswire); AP Nov 25.

Footer: Putin warns retaliation "inevitable" (said Tue 24 Nov, reported
25 Nov); Sweden PM vote set for 30 Nov (speaker announced Tue 24 Nov);
Thanksgiving storm hits the US East Coast (forecast 25 Nov).
